# DM Summary
Trained to become a Knight and skilled swordsman. Older brother mysteriously disappears, and is presumed dead by father. Cillian has set out to try and find him, refusing to believe that he has died. Secretly, his search is a guise to abdicate his own responsibility of succeeding as family patriarch in his brothers stead.  
### Motivations / Goals / Desires
- Find brother (or what happened to him)
	- Work out a way to avoid becoming the ruler of the house
- Continue to improve martial skill

# Backstory
Second son of the once proud O Faol'ain house, **Cillian** grew up in a relatively lavish lifestyle.
Having no interest in politics or statecraft, **Cillian** trained in the art of combat, receiving tutelage in may different disciplines of war. His main goal is to become one of the most skilled swordsmen in the land, and become a lord sworn knight to his older brother **Cathal**, who would ascend their father's throne.

He has a rudimentary understanding of the history of the gods and the mythos surrounding the formation of the island, but adheres to the warrior traditions and prayers that have been passed down from his tutors. 

His plans were skuttled though, after the mysterious disappearance of **Cathal**. Despite there being no body recovered, and only spurious evidence, **Cathal** was presumed dead - their father **Magnus** even holding a funeral him. The gravity of the situation increased as Magnus began to prepare **Cillian** to become the new patriarch. Meetings and introductions with other houses, politics and back scratching, all combined with a complete erasure of **Cathal**, pushed **Cillian** to abandon his home - convinced that **Cathal** must still be alive, and intent on finding him. 

Whether he is driven by the desire to reunite with his older brother, or the desperation to not take his place as the ruler of the house is unclear.
